# Ogden 45-Step Course — Source Map and App Coverage Audit

## 1. Scope and source status

This document is a project-level content map of the 45-step HTML course reproduced by BasicEnglish.org. Its course index labels it “THE ORIGINAL BASIC ENGLISH COURSE by Charles K. Ogden (1930)” and links Steps 1–45.

- Course index: https://basicenglish.org/index_htm.html
- Step pages: https://basicenglish.org/step-1/index.html through https://basicenglish.org/step-45/index.html
- In this audit, all 45 individual Step pages were opened and returned readable HTML.
- This establishes access to the online reproduction, not that every line has been independently collated against a surviving 1930 printed copy. The 1930 English book scan has not been acquired, so exact transcription fidelity to the print edition remains unverified.
- The summaries below are concise source-based indexing notes, not verbatim reproductions and not new Ogden chapter titles.
- This is not yet a complete offline mirror: the linked illustrations/images have not all been downloaded, counted, checked or packaged.

## 2. How the course is organized

The following is a useful project-level reading of the source sequence; these are not official module names supplied by the course index.

1. **Steps 1–25 — vocabulary and structure teaching.** The early steps introduce the 850-word system and its structures through short lessons and readings. Step 25 explicitly states that the learner has now learned all the structure of Basic English and that ten more lessons will complete the 850-word vocabulary.
2. **Steps 26–35 — the ten further vocabulary lessons.** These steps continue the vocabulary through topical readings and dialogues. The “ten more lessons” statement at Step 25 aligns with Steps 26–35.
3. **Steps 36–41 — practical reference topics with readings.** Numbers, time, calendar, money, weights and measures are taught and practised.
4. **Steps 42–45 — integrated use and longer reading.** Everyday-service dialogues, a cinema conversation, an accident narrative and cultural reading bring the course to a close.

This sequence matters: Step 25 is not the end of the 45-step course. It marks the end of the source's explicit structure-teaching stage; the vocabulary and applied-reading stages continue.

## 5. Source observations that affect future adaptation

- **Step 25 is a real curriculum boundary.** Its note says that all Basic English structure has been learned at this point, with ten further lessons needed to complete the vocabulary. Future App design should preserve this distinction instead of treating every Step as the same kind of grammar lesson.
- **Step 42 is an applied-use unit.** It contains five situations: railway station, hotel, bank, post office and library. It also includes notes/tests and asks the learner to give an account in Basic of a railway journey. This is evidence that the original 45 Steps already includes applied expression; any supplementary Meaning → Basic track must be described as making that process more explicit and systematic, not as filling a total absence.
- **Step 41 has an index/body title difference.** The index labels the reading “Goes Marketing”; the page's story heading is “Mrs. Jones Does Her Marketing”. Preserve the discrepancy in source records rather than silently rewriting the source title.
- **Currency and cultural details are period-specific and inconsistent in the online reproduction.** Steps 41–42 mix currency references and historical monetary terms. Step 45 contains cultural observations about England. These pages are language-learning sources, not reliable current guides to currency, prices, customs or social conditions.
- **The reading pages are not all mere vocabulary lists.** Several contain notes explaining how familiar words extend to new senses and how fixed combinations work; Steps 33–35 and 42 are especially rich in such explanatory material and exercises.
- **Illustrations remain a separate audit item.** Page-level image links are visible on multiple Steps, but a complete image inventory and local image archive have not yet been made.

## 6. Recommended next project action

Before adapting the 31 remaining Steps, audit the 14 existing Chinese adaptations against their corresponding source pages. Check that each adaptation preserves the source's teaching point, examples, sequence and exercise intent, and clearly labels any project-written explanation or rewritten reading. Record discrepancies first; do not expand the course by inventing extra lesson divisions.

After that source-fidelity pass, use the 45-step map to choose the next unadapted Step based on prerequisite order and practical learning value. Do not start a new Android build merely for this research document.
